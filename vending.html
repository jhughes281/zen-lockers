Before Placement

What we agree in writing

Before a machine goes in, we put the details in writing so there are no surprises for your property or your residents.

Location & traffic

Where the machine goes, how much foot traffic it sees, and the clearances it needs.

Power

A dedicated outlet near the spot, and who handles any electrical work.

Stocking & service

How often it's restocked and cleaned, what goes in it, and how residents report a problem.

Payments

Card, tap-to-pay, and app checkout, plus who handles refunds.

Terms

Who owns the machine, any cost to the property, and how either side can end the placement.

Certifications

Safety listings belong to the machine's manufacturer. We confirm them for the specific model placed.
