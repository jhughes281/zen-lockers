The business case

The package problem, in three exhibits.

Published figures from the organizations that track parcel volume and package theft. Every exhibit links to its source.

Exhibit 1 · Volume

US parcel volume has doubled since 2016.

Carriers moved 23.1 billion parcels in 2025, up from 11.5 billion in 2016. That works out to 171 parcels per household in a year, and every one of them has to land somewhere on your property.

Source: Pitney Bowes Parcel Shipping Index, 2026 report. Estimates of US parcels up to 70 lb.

Billions of parcels shipped in the US, per year

Exhibit 2 · Exposure

Apartment residents report theft far more often than homeowners.

In a 2026 survey, 45% of apartment residents said they had had a package stolen, against 25% in single-family homes. Over the previous three months the gap was 10% to 3%.

Share who have ever had a package stolen

Source: Security.org, 2026 Package Theft Report. Online survey of 3,494 US adults, September 2026.

Exhibit 3 · Location

Houston ranks fourth among US metros for package theft losses.

SafeWise estimates about $200 million in stolen packages across the Houston metro in its 2025 report, up one place from fifth the year before.

How buying works

From quote to first delivery.

No sales funnel and no call center. You talk to the person who will be on site for the install.

STEP 1

Request a quote

Tell us the property type, unit count or daily order volume, and where the lockers would live. We reply within one business day.

STEP 2

Measure and plan

We visit, measure, check power and network, and recommend a door mix and placement. You get a drawing and a fixed price.

STEP 3

Install and onboard

Hardware installed, software configured, staff and residents onboarded. We stay until the first packages go in.

STEP 4

Local support

Service from Houston, not a ticket queue. If a door sticks or a screen goes dark, you call the people who installed it.

Pricing: hardware, installation, and software are itemized on every quote. Lead time: confirmed at quote, based on configuration and freight.
