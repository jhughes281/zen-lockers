Service Tiers

Flexible pickup
on your schedule

Property managers set the schedule once. Residents get door-to-door convenience. Zen Lockers handles every pickup, route, and removal — one contract, one contact.

01
Weekly
Standard Weekly Pickup

Door-to-door trash collection on a fixed day each week. Property manager sets the route — residents set out their bin, we do the rest.

  • Fixed collection day per property
  • Resident door-to-door service
  • Transport to on-site dumpster
  • Resident pickup reminders included
02
Bi-Weekly
Bi-Weekly Pickup

A lighter-footprint plan for smaller properties or lower-volume communities. Same trusted door-to-door service — every two weeks.

  • Every 14 days, same day each cycle
  • Ideal for smaller complexes
  • Flexible upgrade to weekly anytime
  • Same crew and route consistency
03
Monthly · Heavy Items
Monthly Heavy Item Removal

Furniture, appliances, large boxes. Residents schedule a pickup window within a designated monthly day. Available curbside or in-unit by appointment.

  • One scheduled removal day per month
  • Resident-chosen pickup window
  • Curbside or in-unit options
  • Furniture, appliances, bulk items
Process

Simple for
property managers

01
Property sets the schedule

Managers choose frequency, pickup days, and any special notes. Zen Lockers builds the route around your property layout and resident density.

→
02
Residents get notified

Pickup reminders sent ahead of collection day. Heavy item requests submitted through a simple scheduling window — no confusion, no missed pickups.

→
03
Our crew runs the route

Our on-site community crew collects from each door and transports to the designated dumpster or disposal point. No resident lifting required.

→
04
Monthly reporting

Service summaries delivered to property management each month — routes completed, items collected, and any flagged issues resolved proactively.
